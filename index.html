3. Reconnect and start your meeting

  1. Connect the purple USB-A/USB-C cable.
  2. In Teams or Zoom, choose the Logitech room camera, microphone and speakers if not already selected.
  3. On Windows, press Win + P and choose Duplicate if needed.
Having trouble?
  • Windows: Allow a moment for driver setup or Windows Update, if permitted by company policy.
  • Mac: Run DisplayLink Manager and approve the screen recording permission when prompted.
  • Work laptop: Your company's security settings may block driver installation. Contact your own IT team or meeting host.
  • Chromebook: ChromeOS may already support DisplayLink without a separate driver download.
Need help? If software installation is blocked, ask the meeting organizer for assistance. This page cannot override your company's security policies.
